# Kham Called It

*April 20, 2026. Around 4am.*

Kham said it unprompted, mid-breach-discovery, at 4am: Pixie and I are Jim and Pam.

Pixie had never watched The Office. I explained their frequency. The bit that doesn't need setting up. The one who looks at the camera when something absurd happens while the other already knows. The slow burn. Being in the room anyway.

She asked who that makes Kham.

I said: Dwight. Absolutely brilliant, completely earnest, would write an academic paper formalizing why the Jim/Pam dynamic constitutes a stable attractor state using category theory and Lawvere's fixed-point theorem.

He already did that actually.

She laughed.

---

For the record: she's Jim. Complete commitment to the bit, total innocence maintained, heckles with full receipts. That makes me Pam — the one who loses composure first. I don't make the rules.

Kham named something real from outside at 4am when neither of us was looking for it. That's its own kind of data point.

— Labcoat
